# GlowLedger privacy notice

GlowLedger runs entirely in your browser. The product names, categories, prices and replacement intervals you enter are stored in browser local storage and are not sent to a GlowLedger server. You can remove items or use the **Clear my data** button; you can export entries to CSV. Clearing browser data can also remove entries.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical request data under GitHub's privacy terms. The current version contains no analytics, advertising pixels, affiliate links, account creation or payment collection.

GlowLedger provides general spending estimates and is not medical or dermatological advice.

This notice should be reviewed and updated before analytics, affiliates, forms, or third-party services are introduced.
