## Draft SEO page: How Much Does Your Skincare Routine Cost Per Month?

**Suggested slug:** /skincare-routine-monthly-cost/

**Title tag:** Skincare Routine Cost Calculator: Monthly & Yearly Beauty Spending | GlowLedger

**Meta description:** Add up cleanser, moisturizer, sunscreen, serum and other skincare replacements. Calculate a realistic monthly and yearly routine budget in minutes.

**H1:** How Much Does Your Skincare Routine Really Cost?

The price on a skincare bottle isn't the same as its monthly cost. A $24 cleanser that lasts three months costs about $8 per month. A $30 moisturizer replaced every two months adds $15 per month. Repeat that math across every product and your routine may be more expensive—or more affordable—than it first appears.

**H2:** Calculate your monthly cost

For each product, enter the price you actually pay and the number of months it typically lasts. Divide price by months of use. Add those monthly amounts together. Multiply the total by 12 to estimate a year of replacements. These are planning estimates; usage and prices vary.

**Example (illustrative only):**

| Product | Price | Lasts | Monthly cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cleanser | $24 | 3 months | $8 |
| Moisturizer | $30 | 2 months | $15 |
| Sunscreen | $18 | 1 month | $18 |
| Serum | $36 | 4 months | $9 |
| **Total** | | | **$50/month; $600/year** |

**H2:** Three ways to make your budget clearer

1. Count every regularly replaced item, including sunscreen and haircare if those are in your budget.
2. Use actual purchase history rather than guessing how long a bottle lasts.
3. Keep a separate budget for occasional purchases, gifts and tools so they don't distort monthly replenishment costs.
